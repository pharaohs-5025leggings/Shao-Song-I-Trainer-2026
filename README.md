## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Shao Song I**. This trainer enhances Shao Song I by simplifying quick-time events, expanding control over branching story progress, and providing convenient options for tactical strategy segments. It is designed for players who want to explore alternate outcomes, replay key decisions, and reduce repetitive challenges.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore every story branch, experience alternate endings, replay tactical sequences more efficiently, or reduce the difficulty of quick-time events.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Shao Song I is primarily a menu-driven interactive narrative rather than a free-roaming action game. Navigation is handled through story menus, dialogue selections, chapter prompts, and tactical interfaces. Movement-related trainer options are therefore not applicable.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not use a conventional open-world inventory system. Progress is centered on story decisions, character information, route conditions, and tactical scenarios. Use story and branch options carefully because altering progress can affect later scenes and endings.

### World & Environment

The story follows Zhao Jiu, a modern college student who becomes Emperor Gaozong of Song, Zhao Gou, during the Southern Song crisis. Decisions made in court, during political conflicts, and across the northern campaign shape the available routes and conclusions.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is presented through strategic and turn-based tactical sequences rather than continuous action combat. Trainer options can assist with supported tactical encounters or compatibility them, while quick-time event assistance applies to interactive cinematic scenes.
